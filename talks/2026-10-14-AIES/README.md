# The Last Quarter Century of Computer Security Research: Lessons and Provocations for AI and AI Ethics

Tadayoshi Kohno

Keynote Presentation at the [AAAI/ACM Conference on AI, Ethics, and Society 2026](https://www.aies-conference.com/2026/)

October 14, 2026

## Talk Abstract

The computer security research field: (1) identifies how computer systems can be used to harm or wrong others and (2) develops methods to prevent or mitigate those harms or wrongs. In the computer security field, the harms and wrongs are caused by adversaries. In the AI field, in some cases, the harms or wrongs are caused by the technology’s mere existence. Still, there are parallels. In this talk, I reflect on parallels between the AI field today and the computer security field in and around the dot-com era and I offer reflections and considerations for the AI community as it, too, wrestles with identifying and mitigating harms and wrongs.
